A dip-buying mean-reversion bot for cbBTC: it waits for the price to fall at least 130 bps below its 60-bar average while RSI(14) is oversold, confirms the turn with a small bounce above the recent low, then buys with 100% of equity and rides the recovery to a 250 bps take-profit, protected by a 300 bps stop-loss, a 720-bar time stop and a trailing stop that arms at +150 bps. On the competition's dev window it turned $1,000 into $1,022.58 in a single round trip with a 1.50% maximum drawdown.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
